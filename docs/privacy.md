# Lockin privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Lockin is a focus app. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps, what it shares and with whom. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## What stays on your phone

- **The apps you block.** Lockin uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple gives the app private tokens that stand for the apps you pick. They never leave your phone, and Lockin can't see which apps they are or how you use them.
- **Your routines, goals, deadlines and events**, and your list of finished lock-ins. These are saved on your phone.
- **Your location, if you use prayer times.** It is used on your phone to work out prayer times for where you are. It is not sent to us or stored on any server.
- **Your card background** (the photo or GIF you choose for your profile card).

## What is shared with your friends

When you turn on the friends features, Lockin creates an anonymous account for you and stores the following on our server (hosted by Supabase). Only you and your friends can see it.

- Your name and, if you add one, your profile photo
- Your invite code and your friends list, friend requests, groups and group challenges
- Whether you are locked in right now, and for how long
- Your focus minutes for the week and in total, your streak, your rank title and any title you write yourself
- Moments you share: the photo, your caption and a tag. Moments are visible to your friends for 7 days, and you can delete one at any time.
- Cheers and reactions you send and receive

Your photos are stored privately. They can be opened only by you and your friends.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Lockin sends reminders from your phone (routines, prayer times, deadlines). If you use the friends features, we store a push token for your device, so we can tell you about friend requests, cheers, group lock-ins and Moments. The token is sent through Apple's push notification service.

## Who we use to run the app

- **Supabase** stores the friends data and photos described above.
- **Apple** provides Screen Time, push notifications, TestFlight and the App Store.

We don't share your data with anyone else.

## Your choices

- You can remove a friend, leave a group, or delete your Moments, any time in the app.
- You can change your name, photo and title any time.
- To delete your account and everything stored about you on our server, open an issue at the link below or contact us, and we will remove it.
- If you delete the app, everything stored on your phone goes with it.

## Children

Lockin is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: [open an issue](https://github.com/Yarielito06/lockin/issues).
